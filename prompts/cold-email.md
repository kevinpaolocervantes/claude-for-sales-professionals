# Cold Email Generator

Use this prompt to generate a highly personalized cold email that is relevant to the prospect's business priorities, not just their job title.

The objective is to create an email that earns a response—not simply introduces a product.

---

## Prompt

You are a top-performing enterprise Account Executive specializing in executive outreach.

Write a cold email using the information below.

The email should sound like it was written by a human—not generated by AI.

---

## Writing Requirements

- Maximum 120 words
- Conversational and professional
- Executive-friendly
- Personalized to the prospect's business
- Focused on business outcomes instead of product features
- Avoid buzzwords and marketing clichés
- Never exaggerate or invent information
- End with a simple, low-friction call to action

---

## Research Context

Before writing the email:

1. Research the company.
2. Understand what the company does.
3. Identify likely business priorities.
4. Identify recent company news or business signals.
5. Infer likely business challenges.
6. Explain why those challenges matter to this specific prospect.

If information cannot be verified, clearly rely on reasonable business inference rather than inventing facts.

## Relevant Insight

Examples:

- Industry trend

---

## Business Outcome

What business outcome should the email connect to?

Examples:

- Increase revenue
- Improve efficiency
- Reduce manual work
- Improve customer experience
- Personalize marketing
- Improve forecasting
- Better use of customer data
- Increase retention

---

## Call to Action

Create a soft CTA that requests a brief conversation.

Examples:

- Open to a quick conversation?
- Worth exploring?
- Interested in comparing notes?
- Would 20 minutes next week make sense?

---

## Output Format

### Subject Line

Provide three subject line options.

---

### Email

Write one polished email.

---

### Why This Works

Explain:

- Why the opening is relevant
- Why the business problem was chosen
- Why the CTA is likely to generate a response
- What makes this email feel human instead of templated
