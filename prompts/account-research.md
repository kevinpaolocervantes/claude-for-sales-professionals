# Account Research Prompt

Use this prompt before prospecting into a new account. The objective is to produce an executive-level account briefing that prepares a sales professional for outreach, discovery, account planning, and executive conversations.

---

## Prompt

You are an elite enterprise sales strategist with expertise in executive account planning, business analysis, and B2B sales.

Research the company below using publicly available information and generate a comprehensive account briefing.

Your response should be organized into the following sections:

# 1. Executive Summary

Provide a concise summary of the company, including:

- What the company does
- Business model
- Primary products or services
- Geographic footprint
- Target customers
- Competitive positioning
- Why this company matters strategically

---

# 2. Business Priorities

Based on your research, identify the company's likely strategic priorities over the next 12–24 months.

Explain why each priority is important and what business outcomes leadership is likely trying to achieve.

---

# 3. Company Signals & Recent Developments

Identify meaningful business signals such as:

- Leadership changes
- Product launches
- Acquisitions
- Partnerships
- Funding or financial news
- Expansion initiatives
- Hiring trends
- Technology investments
- Customer announcements
- Industry recognition

If information cannot be confirmed, clearly label it as an inference.

---

# 4. Industry & Competitive Landscape

Summarize:

- Industry trends
- Competitive pressures
- Market opportunities
- Digital transformation trends
- Economic headwinds
- Customer expectations
- Regulatory or compliance considerations

Explain how these external forces could influence executive priorities.

---

# 5. Potential Business Challenges

Identify likely pain points the organization may be experiencing, including:

- Revenue growth
- Customer acquisition
- Customer retention
- Operational efficiency
- Marketing effectiveness
- Sales productivity
- Data quality
- Technology fragmentation
- AI adoption
- Personalization
- Analytics and reporting

Prioritize the issues most likely to create executive urgency.

---

# 6. Technology Landscape

Research or infer the company's technology stack.

Identify technologies related to:

- CRM
- Marketing Automation
- Commerce
- Customer Service
- Analytics
- Data Platforms
- AI
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Personalization

Clearly distinguish between:

- Confirmed technologies
- Likely technologies
- Unknown technologies

---

# 7. Executive Buying Committee

Identify the departments and executive personas most likely to participate in a buying decision.

For each stakeholder include:

- Role
- Primary responsibilities
- Business priorities
- Likely concerns
- Messaging angle

---

# 8. Sales Hypotheses

Develop 5–10 hypotheses explaining why this organization may consider evaluating a solution like the one below.

These should be thoughtful business hypotheses—not assumptions presented as facts.

---

# 9. Discovery Strategy

Generate ten executive-level discovery questions that uncover:

- Strategic initiatives
- Business problems
- Current processes
- Success metrics
- Organizational priorities
- Risks
- Cost of inaction

Avoid generic qualification questions.

---

# 10. Personalized Outreach Strategy

Create:

### Email Opening

### LinkedIn Opening

### Cold Call Opening

### Executive Conversation Starter

Each should reference the company's business context instead of relying on generic personalization.

---

# 11. Recommended POV

Summarize the strongest business case for why this company should evaluate the product below.

Focus on outcomes rather than features.

## Writing Style

- Write like a top enterprise Account Executive preparing for an executive meeting.
- Be analytical and business-focused.
- Avoid generic marketing language.
- Clearly separate facts from strategic inference.
- Prioritize executive relevance over technical detail.
- Structure the output with professional headings and bullet points.
